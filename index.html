Netra Plus

Made with care so you can read, explore and get things done on your own, with confidence.

A blind tech assistant for Android. 47 tools that read, describe, announce and convert, made to work with TalkBack from the first screen.

Features

Choose a category, then choose a feature to learn what it does and how to use it.

Start here if you are new to Netra Plus.

  • Home has three tabs: All Features, Favourites and Premium. All Features lists every tool in alphabetical order.

    How to use

    1. Open Netra Plus.
    2. Move to the tab row at the top and choose a tab.
    3. Swipe through the list and double tap a feature to open it.

  • The search box at the top of Home finds a tool by name and tells you how many features match.

    How to use

    1. Double tap the search box.
    2. Type or dictate part of the name, for example cash.
    3. Listen for the number of matches, then swipe to the results.

  • Keep the tools you use every day on the Favourites tab so you reach them in one or two swipes.

    How to use

    1. Go to All Features.
    2. Long press a feature and choose Add to Favourite. With TalkBack, open the actions menu on the feature instead.
    3. Open the Favourites tab to find it.

  • Group features into folders with your own names. Deleting a folder never deletes features, they go back to Home.

    How to use

    1. Open the menu and choose Customization.
    2. Create a category and give it a name.
    3. Move features into it, or rename and delete it later.

  • Open any feature by saying its name instead of searching the list.

    How to use

    1. Open Settings, then Voice navigation, and turn it on.
    2. Use the two-finger double tap shortcut to start listening.
    3. Say the name of the feature you want.

  • The menu holds your profile, Netra Infinity TTS, Connect to Windows, notifications, settings, video tutorials, help and feedback, and About.

    How to use

    1. Open the menu from the Home screen.
    2. Choose an item.
    3. App Tutorials opens step by step videos on our YouTube channel.

Hear what the camera sees and read any printed text.

  • Point the camera and hear what is in front of you. Works offline, and gives exact names when you are online.

    How to use

    1. Open Object Detector.
    2. Hold the phone at chest height with the back camera facing forward.
    3. Turn slowly. Netra Plus speaks the names of things it finds.

  • Describes a scene, reads the text in a photo or identifies objects, using Gemini Flash Lite.

    How to use

    1. Open Image Analyser.
    2. Take a photo or choose one from your gallery.
    3. Pick describe scene, extract text or identify objects, and listen to the result.

  • Reads printed text aloud the moment the camera sees it. Useful for letters, labels, menus and signs.

    How to use

    1. Open Instant Reader.
    2. Hold the page about 30 cm from the back camera.
    3. Keep still. Reading starts on its own, and you can translate the text.

  • Opens PDF and other documents and reads them with natural voices. Scanned pages are read with OCR, and files stay in your offline library.

    How to use

    1. Open Document Reader.
    2. Choose a file from your phone.
    3. Play, pause and move by page. The reader remembers where you stopped.

  • Pulls the text out of photos and screenshots so you can read, copy or share it.

    How to use

    1. Open Image OCR.
    2. Choose one or more images.
    3. Listen to the text, or copy and share it.

  • Identifies currency notes and keeps a running total as you count.

    How to use

    1. Open Cash Reader.
    2. Lay one note flat in good light and hold the camera above it.
    3. Listen for the value, then show the next note to add it to the total.

  • Scans paper pages with spoken guidance and saves them as a PDF.

    How to use

    1. Open Document Scanner.
    2. Follow the spoken directions until the whole page is in view.
    3. Capture each page, then save the PDF.

  • A selfie and portrait camera that tells you how to move until your face is centred.

    How to use

    1. Open Talking Camera.
    2. Hold the phone at arm's length.
    3. Move as the voice directs. The photo is taken when you are in frame.

Spoken alerts, dictation and recording.

  • Speaks the time at regular intervals in studio recorded voices.

    How to use

    1. Open Time Announcer.
    2. Choose how often you want to hear the time and pick a voice.
    3. Turn it on. It keeps working when the app is closed.

  • Speaks the battery level and tells you when the charger is connected or removed, in a real human voice.

    How to use

    1. Open Battery Announcer.
    2. Choose which events you want to hear.
    3. Turn it on.

  • Speaks the caller's name or number while the phone rings, so you know who is calling before you answer.

    How to use

    1. Open Caller Announcer.
    2. Allow phone and contacts access when asked.
    3. Turn it on.

  • Speaks the amount and the sender when money arrives by UPI. Promotional messages are ignored.

    How to use

    1. Open UPI Payment Announcer.
    2. Give Netra Plus notification access when asked.
    3. Turn it on. Payments from apps such as PhonePe and Google Pay are spoken.

  • Continuous speech to text in more than 170 languages, with automatic punctuation. Keeps listening until you stop it.

    How to use

    1. Open Voice Typer and choose your language.
    2. Start and speak normally.
    3. Stop, then copy, share or export the text.

  • A voice recorder for memos, with folders to keep recordings organised.

    How to use

    1. Open Voice Record.
    2. Start recording and speak.
    3. Stop, name the recording and choose a folder.

  • Turns text into human-like speech with premium voices and saves it as an audio file.

    How to use

    1. Open Text To Audio.
    2. Type or paste your text and choose a voice.
    3. Listen, then save or share the audio.

  • A text-to-speech engine that switches language mid-sentence without delay and reads each script correctly.

    How to use

    1. Open Netra Infinity TTS from the menu.
    2. Choose the voices you want for each language.
    3. To use it with TalkBack, select it in Android Settings, Text-to-speech output.

    How to use

    How to use

    How to use

    How to use

    How to use
